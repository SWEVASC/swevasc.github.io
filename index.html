Swedish ANCA Vasculitis Network

A national multidisciplinary network advancing research, collaboration, education, and clinical care for patients with ANCA-associated vasculitis in Sweden.

About the Network

The Swedish ANCA Vasculitis Network brings together clinicians, researchers, healthcare professionals, and patient representatives with the shared goal of improving outcomes for individuals living with ANCA-associated vasculitis.

Our Mission

Research

Facilitate national collaborative studies and strengthen translational and clinical vasculitis research.

Education

Share expertise through seminars, meetings, guidelines and educational resources.

Patient Care

Promote equitable, evidence-based care across Sweden and improve long-term outcomes.
