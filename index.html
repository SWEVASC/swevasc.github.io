SweVasc

A collaborative initiative of researchers, physicians and nurses across Sweden to advance research and improve outcomes for people with ANCA-associated vasculitis.

About the network Meet the members

Background and need

Anti-neutrophil cytoplasmic antibody (ANCA)-associated vasculitides (AAV) are severe, relapsing-remitting autoimmune diseases associated with high morbidity and increased mortality. Current treatments rely heavily on broad immunosuppression, which carries substantial risks such as severe infections. There is a clear clinical need for safer, more targeted treatment strategies, standardized follow-up routines and a more uniform national care structure.

AAV are rare (prevalence 200–400 per million), so Swedish clinics manage small and heterogeneous patient cohorts. This leads to fragmented clinical practice, limited opportunities for methodologically robust studies, and difficulty translating research advances into consistent care. A coordinated national research and clinical network is therefore essential, both for scientific understanding and for standardized clinical management.

Our mission

The network will deliver clinically relevant research collaboration and concrete, implementable outputs for Swedish rheumatology and nephrology.

  1. Strengthen collaborative translational and clinical studies with direct implications for patient management.
  2. Create shared research protocols for harmonized data collection, biosampling and outcome definitions across Swedish centres.
  3. Support junior researchers through structured mentoring, presentation opportunities and integration into collaborative projects.
  4. Incorporate patient research partners to ensure that network-generated guidelines and research protocols align with patient priorities.

How we work

Annual in-person meetings, starting in autumn 2026, serve as focused workshops. Regular online coordination keeps the work moving between meetings.

Annual meetings

  1. Coordinated updates on collaborative studies
  2. Development of clinical and research protocols
  3. Scientific exchange and mentorship for junior researchers
  4. Structured feedback from patient research partners

Long-term outcomes

  • Stronger multicentre collaboration and junior researcher development
  • New therapeutic targets and improved access to multicentre trials
  • A sustained national platform for evidence-based, uniform AAV care across Sweden
