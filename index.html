About SWEVASC

SWEVASC is a newly established national network dedicated to ANCA-associated vasculitis. The network aims to strengthen collaboration between Swedish academic centres, healthcare professionals, researchers and patient representatives.

Objectives

  • • Facilitate national research collaborations
  • • Promote high-quality patient care
  • • Support education and training
  • • Develop multicentre studies and registries
  • • Engage patient research partners

Research Priorities

Clinical Studies

National observational and interventional projects.

Biomarkers

Identification of prognostic and disease activity markers.

Precision Medicine

Improved risk stratification and personalized treatment.

Patient Outcomes

Long-term health, quality of life and complications.
